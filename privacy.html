Privacy notice

Last updated 27 October 2026

What we store

Nothing, if you use the site as a guest: there are no ads, no trackers and no analytics. Your settings (theme, filters) stay in your own browser. If you create an account we store your email address, the display name you choose, and what you save: the coins you follow, your notes on them and your dashboard settings. If you sign in with Google we receive your name and email from Google, nothing else.

Where

Accounts are run by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Your password is never stored by us in readable form; Supabase keeps only a salted hash. Every saved row is protected so only your own signed-in account can read or change it.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, send marketing emails, or use your data to train anything. Emails are sent only to confirm your account or reset your password.

Other services the page loads

To display the page your browser fetches fonts from Google Fonts and two pinned code libraries (charts, sign-in) from unpkg.com, and the page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, these services see your IP address; we receive nothing from them.

Your control

From My dashboard you can download everything we hold about you (Export my data), sign out of all devices, and delete your account. Deleting removes your account and all saved items at once, for good.

Contact

Questions or data requests: gm@koreit.io.
